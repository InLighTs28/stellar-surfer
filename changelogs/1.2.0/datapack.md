## ✦ 1.2.0

- **Trials rebuilt**: they fail if you die or leave the arena, their creatures always disappear, 3 attempts per shrine, a different consequence for each failed trial, shrines only in Fallen Altar structures, no more mote duplication.
- **Cosmic Body**: immune to fire, lava, freezing and poison, breathes underwater. Sun passive: Solar Flares +1 s.
- **Hunted** is now a chance per hit (100 % at Rank 0, down to 15 % at Rank 10).
- **Faster Stellar Breath charge** at every Rank; **free float faster with Rank**.
- New Neptune skill **Infinite Distance** (projectiles stop in mid-air, monsters crawl).
- **Modpack difficulty** detection (L2 Hostility, Enhanced AI, Cataclysm, Born in Chaos, Iron's Spells...): more Stardust, cosmic damage and health in harder packs. Enhanced AI zombies no longer bite back against cosmic damage.

Requires **Stellar Surfer — Cosmic Visuals 1.2.0**. Found a bug? https://github.com/InLighTs28/stellar-surfer/issues
